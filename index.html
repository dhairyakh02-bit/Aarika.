03 / Experience

The journey so far.

Every experience has offered a different lesson in leadership, service and finding the confidence to contribute.

AUG 2026 — PRESENT
NEW DELHI, INDIA

Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs

Best Speaker · 58th Youth Parliament
Government of India

For the 58th Youth Parliament, my school was awarded first prize and I was recognised as Best Speaker by the Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs. We were invited to the New Parliament building in New Delhi to repeat our showcase before dignitaries and members of Parliament. I served as Speaker and led proceedings addressing issues including deepfake prevention, regulation of online money gaming apps and breach of privilege. The experience offered a closer understanding of deliberation and the importance of representing citizens' voices.

DEC 2025 — PRESENT
DELHI, INDIA

National Cadet Corps (NCC)

NCC Cadet · A-Certificate Holder
India

An actively participating cadet and A-Certificate holder. Regular unit activities, early morning drills, sports competitions and interactive lectures have strengthened my understanding of discipline, unity and camaraderie.

OCT 2025 — PRESENT
NEW DELHI, INDIA

SarvHope Society

Founder
Student-led social impact initiative

Founded on the belief that opportunity should never be determined by circumstance, SarvHope Society works to bridge gaps in social awareness through performing arts. The initiative aims to empower young people through fundraising, volunteer engagement, community partnerships and awareness campaigns. My work includes communications, event and project planning, workshops and execution with a team of student volunteers.

APR 2024 — PRESENT
DELHI, INDIA

Interact District 3012

Member · Interact Club
Rotary-sponsored youth leadership and service organisation

Participated in community initiatives including blood donation drives, animal shelter visits, workshops in government schools and fundraisers. The experience continues to reinforce the importance of empathy and giving back to the community.

DEC 2024 — OCT 2025
DELHI, INDIA

The World Scholar's Cup

Regional Qualifier
Team and individual competitions

Across two participating years, my team earned Second Overall Regional Qualifiers, Second Overall in the Scholars Challenge Examination and Fourth Overall in Creative Writing. I also received twelve and nine individual medals in the respective years. The competition strengthened my debate, critical-thinking and team-coordination skills.
